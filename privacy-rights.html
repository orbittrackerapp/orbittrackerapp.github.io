Your Privacy Rights

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Developer: Said Koca

This page explains your rights over your personal data wherever you live, and how to use them. What data is processed and why is described in the privacy policy.

1. Who is responsible

Said Koca, an individual developer (not a company), is responsible for your personal data in connection with Orbit. Contact: orbittrackerapp@gmail.com

2. Rights for everyone

Wherever you live, you can: ask whether we hold personal data about you and get a copy; have it corrected or deleted; withdraw any permission you gave; and ask questions about how your data is handled.

Most of this you can do yourself in the app, because your tasks, times and notes are stored only on your device: Settings → Export my data (a copy of everything), edit or delete single entries, Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app. Advertising choices: Settings → Ad choices, or iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3. EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing (Art. 7(3)). You can object to processing based on legitimate interest, including non-personalized ads, at any time.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work.

4. Türkiye (KVKK)

Under Article 11 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK) you have the right to: learn whether your data is processed and request information about it; learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; request correction; request deletion or destruction under Article 7; request that these be notified to recipients; object to a result against you arising exclusively from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing.

Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest (Article 13 KVKK). If your request is rejected or not answered, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu). The privacy policy covers the information required by Article 10 KVKK (purposes, legal grounds, transfers including abroad, method of collection).

5. United States, including California

Depending on your state (for example California under the CCPA/CPRA, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to access, correct and delete it, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising.

We do not sell personal information for money. Showing personalized ads through Google AdMob may count as “sharing” for targeted advertising. To opt out, decline the tracking request or turn off tracking for Orbit in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; ads are then not personalized with your advertising identifier. We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights.

6. Brazil and other countries

Users in Brazil have the rights of Article 18 LGPD (confirmation, access, correction, anonymization or deletion, portability, information about sharing, and withdrawal of consent). Users elsewhere have the rights given by their local law; we honor such requests in the same way.

7. How to make a request

Write to orbittrackerapp@gmail.com with your request. Because we do not hold an account for you, we may ask for information that helps us find what you are asking about (for example the date and subject of an earlier email). We answer free of charge, within 30 days at the latest; where your local law allows a longer period (for example 45 days in California) we will tell you if we need it.

Your entries in the app are not stored by us, so we cannot see, copy or delete them for you; use the in-app options above. For advertising data held by Google, you can also contact Google directly (policies.google.com/privacy).
