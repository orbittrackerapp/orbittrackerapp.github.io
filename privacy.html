Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Developer: Said Koca

In short: everything you enter (tasks, times, notes) stays on your device. We have no servers and no accounts. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob; for this, Google processes certain device data as described in section 4. Personalized ads are only shown with your permission.

1. Controller

Said Koca
orbittrackerapp@gmail.com

Orbit is published by an individual developer, not a company. If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you. Support emails are handled through Google’s email service (Gmail) and are deleted no later than two years after your request has been resolved.

2. What the app stores

Task names you enter, the start and end times of your work and break entries, notes you add to entries, your settings (such as break length and reminders) and your language choice.

This data is stored only in the app’s local storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and we cannot access it.

3. What the app does not do

No account or sign-in. The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. Your tasks, times and notes are never shared with Google or anyone else, and we do not sell your data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Summary and Settings screens show banner ads from Google AdMob. The provider is Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) for users in the EEA and Switzerland, and Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA) elsewhere.

To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google’s software in the app processes data such as the device’s advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) or other device identifiers, your IP address and an approximate location derived from it, the ads shown to you and your interactions with them (for example taps), and technical and performance data. Your tasks, times and notes are not part of this.

Personalized ads only with your permission: iOS asks whether Orbit may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized on that basis. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choices first. You can change your choices at any time under Settings → Ad choices or in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy; how Google uses data for ads: policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and may process it outside your country, including in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and also uses the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

5. Notifications and calendar

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example before a break ends) are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No push server is used and no device token is collected. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app’s settings or in iPhone Settings.

If you use “Add to Calendar”, iOS asks for calendar access. The app uses it only to list your calendars so you can choose one, to add the entries you export as events and to update events it added earlier. Other events in your calendar are not read and nothing is sent to us. Added events live in your calendar; if that calendar syncs with an account such as iCloud, Google or Outlook, the sync is governed by that provider’s terms. You can withdraw access at any time in iPhone Settings.

The Lock Screen timer (Live Activity) and the Home Screen widget show the running task’s name, timer and up to four task names. To display them, the app shares this information with its own widget on your device only, through an app group. Anyone who can see your screen may see it; you can turn the Lock Screen timer off in Settings and remove the widget at any time.

6. Sharing and export

When you share a summary, export to Excel or export your data, you choose the receiving app (e.g. Mail). The data is then handled under that app’s privacy policy. Nothing is shared without your action.

7. Backups and crash reports

If iCloud Backup or computer backups are turned on, iOS may include the app’s data in your device backup. These backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

If you have chosen in iPhone Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. We do not collect these ourselves.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can edit or delete single entries, delete all data under Settings → Data, or delete the app, which removes all of its data from your device. Advertising data processed by Google is retained according to Google’s policies.

9. Legal basis

Your entries are processed only on your device to provide the features you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Notifications and calendar access are only used with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Personalized ads and access to the advertising identifier are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 25(1) TDDDG in Germany). Showing non-personalized ads is based on our legitimate interest in offering the app free of charge (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time under Settings → Ad choices; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Turkish KVKK you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data and to object to processing. Since the data is on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app (export, edit, delete).

For any question, write to orbittrackerapp@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (e.g. your local supervisory authority in the EU, or the Personal Data Protection Authority in Türkiye).

Your rights by region (GDPR, KVKK, U.S. state laws and others) and how to make a request are explained on the “Your privacy rights” page (Settings → Privacy and legal).

11. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be available in the app and at the address listed in the App Store. The date at the top shows the latest version.
