Orbit

Tasks overlap, your time doesn’t. An iPhone app that tracks how long each task takes with one tap and counts breaks separately.

Support and contact

For questions, feedback or to report a problem: orbittrackerapp@gmail.com

Your tasks, times and notes are stored only on your phone. To export them, open the app → Settings → Export my data; to delete everything, use Settings → Delete all data.

Moving to a new iPhone

On your old iPhone, open Settings → Export my data and save the backup file to Files or iCloud Drive. On your new iPhone, install Orbit and open Settings → Restore from backup, then choose that file. The backup replaces the tasks and entries on the new phone; right after restoring you can tap Undo.
